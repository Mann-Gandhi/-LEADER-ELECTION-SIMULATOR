How the Bully Algorithm works

  1. Every process has a unique ID.
  2. A process notices that the leader has failed.
  3. It starts an election.
  4. It sends ELECTION messages to every process with a higher ID.
  5. Active higher-ID processes reply OK and start their own elections.
  6. The highest active process gets no reply from above, so it becomes leader.
  7. The new leader sends a COORDINATOR message to everyone.
